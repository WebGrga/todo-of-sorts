# Approved stamp direction

The reference is `approved-stamp-preview.html`. Changes to the native app should preserve its composition, not merely its palette.

- Compact date and sound row. Date opens day navigation; secondary actions live under the footer menu.
- Closely set, two-line condensed headline: CONSIDER / IT DONE.
- Monospaced ledger and metadata. Segmented commitment meter.
- One continuous stack of numbered paper slips. No extra section headings ahead of tasks.
- Paper has slight alternating rotations, irregular lower edges, warm shadows, and dashed separators.
- Completion lands on the lower right of the same slip. Impact, paper compression, ink flecks, and score update happen together.
- Completed slips remain legible and in place. Undo remains accessible.
- Navigation is a thin printed footer, with no large filled tabs competing with the stamp.
- Screen comparisons must cover pending tasks, completed tasks, empty days, the editor, and the final seal. Functional test results do not establish visual fidelity.

The native fonts are Anton, Open Sans, and IBM Plex Mono, bundled for offline use. Native input controls retain their behavior inside paper-colored fields.
